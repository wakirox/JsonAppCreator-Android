Describe an app in a JSON file and JSON App Creator runs it as a native Android app. No Android Studio, no build, nothing to compile.

WHAT YOU CAN BUILD
• Screens with text, buttons and images from the web, in vertical and horizontal layouts
• Lists, written in the file or loaded from a REST API, with a detail screen for each item
• Forms with text, number, email, password, date, choice and switch fields, and required fields
• Navigation between screens, alerts, and GET or POST requests whose response opens the next screen
• Events added to your calendar, filled in from a form
• Data binding with ${placeholders}

A BUILT-IN EDITOR
Write and change apps on your phone: syntax colours, suggestions for what each part can still contain (required ones first), a live check that points at every mistake, ready-made views to insert, and one tap to run.

MAKE IT WITH YOUR AI APP
JSON App Creator has no AI inside. It hands complete instructions to the AI app you already use, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or Claude. Chat there, then bring the result back: open the file it makes, share or paste its answer, or share a link to the chat. The app opens it checked and ready to run, and can send any problems back to your AI app for a fix.

YOUR APPS ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Every app you make is kept on the home page, and each one can go on your phone's home screen with its own icon.

READY-MADE EXAMPLES
Start from a gallery of famous paintings, a race sign-up form, a Pokédex loaded from a public API or a movie catalogue, and change any of them in the editor.

YOUR API KEYS STAY ON YOUR PHONE
If an app needs an API key, JSON App Creator asks for it once and keeps it encrypted on your phone, tied to that app. It is never written into the JSON.

OPEN SOURCE, NO ACCOUNT
No ads, no account, no data collected. JSON App Creator is open source under the MIT licence; the format reference, a guide for AI assistants and examples are on GitHub: github.com/wakirox/JsonAppCreator-Android
